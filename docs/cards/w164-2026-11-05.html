Implications
  1. Solo WebinarJam lost the show edge. On the two dual-tool weeks, the WebinarJam half showed about 23% of its regs. This week WebinarJam ran alone and showed only 9.2% on 3,067 regs. Live still grew to 283 from the ~134 WebinarJam half last week, but regs scaled much faster, so the rate looks like Demio again. Dig into whether the bigger invite mix or the join flow is what crushed show.
  2. Reg conversion finally looked healthy on WebinarJam. 262,237 invites produced 3,067 regs, a 1.2% reg rate — close to Demio’s old pace and far above the 0.4–0.5% the WebinarJam half posted when it shared the week. That is the good news. The bad news is those regs did not show, so fix attendance before celebrating the sign-up lift.
  3. Nonjoiners are still half the room on paper. 1,517 of 3,067 regs (49%) were re-invites, and Studio’s scorecard puts their attend rate around 2% versus about 11.5% for net-new. That mix is quietly capping show. Keep pushing fresh and recycled net-new so the next WebinarJam-only week is not half re-invites.
  4. Books are early and dual-counted. 14 distinct people booked (Book B 12 pipeline rows), a 7.2% Book % against the 194 who stayed ten minutes. That sits between W162’s 6.0% and W163’s refreshed 10.1%. Confirm the WebinarJam tape and chat still point at the booking link; books usually keep rising for a few days.
  5. This card landed on a Friday retry. The invite ledger, nonjoiner CSV, deep sync, and Studio scorecard only cleared Friday morning after the Thursday webinar. If WebinarJam-only weeks keep this lag, move the report generate + deep sync into Thursday evening so the next card can publish on time.

Implications
  1. Show got worse on the second solo WebinarJam week. Last week’s Thursday W164 showed 9.2% of 3,067 regs with 283 live. This Tuesday showed 4.9% of 2,630 regs with only 128 live — well below the 12% floor, and about half last week’s attendance. The drop is not just a smaller invite file; fewer of the people who registered actually joined. Dig into the join flow, reminder cadence, and whether moving back to Tuesday changed when people showed up.
  2. The room leaned even harder on nonjoiners. 1,481 of 2,630 regs (56%) were re-invites, up from 49% last week, and Studio’s scorecard puts their attend rate around 1.4% versus about 6.8% for net-new. That mix is quietly capping show. Keep pushing fresh and recycled net-new so the next WebinarJam-only week is not more than half re-invites.
  3. Registration still looked healthy on a smaller send. 207,783 ledger invites produced 2,630 regs, a 1.3% reg rate — in line with last week’s 1.2% and well above the 0.4–0.5% the WebinarJam half posted when it shared the week with Demio. Sign-up is not the broken step. The problem is getting those regs into the room.
  4. Books are early and thin. Only 2 people have booked so far, a 2.4% Book % against the 84 who stayed ten minutes. That sits well below last week’s refreshed 16 people / 8.2% Book % and W163’s 10.1%. Confirm the WebinarJam tape and chat still point at the booking link; books usually keep rising for a few days, but the T10 room itself is less than half of last week’s 194, so even a normal close rate will produce fewer books.

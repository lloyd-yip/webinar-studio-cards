Implications
  1. Show is the emergency this week. The live room shrank from 269 people in W159 to 168 people, even though regs barely moved. People signed up at a normal pace and then did not join. Before W161 on Oct 13, check the W160 reminder emails, texts, and join links end to end to find where people dropped.
  2. Net-new people did not show up either. Studio’s scorecard puts the net-new show rate at about 7.5%, which is about half of W159’s 14.5%. That means the drop is not only the heavy nonjoiner mix; something hit everyone. Treat it as a reminder or join-flow problem first and a list-mix problem second.
  3. The people who joined stayed. About 80% of live stayed ten minutes or more, which is the best stickiness in this set. The content held the room, so fixing attendance should flow straight into more people who hear the offer.
  4. Books are still running at the weak Zoom pace. Only 6 people booked, which is a 4.4% Book % against the people who stayed ten minutes. The WebinarGeek weeks W152 to W157 ran between 8.3% and 15.2%. This matches the Zoom booking problem locked on Sep 30, where the pre-recording never tells people the booking link is in the chat, so confirm the W161 tape and chat call-to-action are fixed before next Tuesday. Books usually keep rising for a few days, so this number will move.

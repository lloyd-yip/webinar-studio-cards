Implications
  1. WebinarJam won the room, but Demio won the sign-ups. WebinarJam showed 22.8% of its regs, more than three times Demio at 6.8%. But WebinarJam only turned 0.41% of its invites into regs, while Demio turned 1.45% into regs. So the two halves ended with almost the same live room, 122 on WebinarJam and 132 on Demio, from about the same number of invites. Before anyone calls a winner, check whether the WebinarJam sign-up step is losing people or whether some of its regs never reached Studio. That matters right now, because next week runs on one tool while Lloyd is away.
  2. Book % fell by more than half. Only 9 people booked, which is a 4.9% Book % against the 182 people who stayed ten minutes. W161 was at 10.8% on WebinarGeek. Neither new tool did well here: Demio is at about 4.6% (4 people) and WebinarJam at about 3.2% (3 people), and 2 bookers did not match either list. This looks like the Sep 30 Zoom lesson again, so confirm both tapes and chats tell people exactly where to book. Books keep rising for a few days, so this number will move.
  3. WebinarJam held people better once they joined. About 78% of the WebinarJam live room stayed ten minutes or more, compared with about 66% on Demio. So the WebinarJam room was not just bigger as a share of regs, it also kept people longer. That is a real point in its favor if the sign-up gap turns out to be fixable.
  4. The Demio half leaned hard on nonjoiners. 1,321 of Demio’s 1,954 regs were people re-invited after missing an earlier webinar, which is about 68% and the highest share in this set. Only 633 Demio regs were net-new. The WebinarJam split is not built in Studio yet, so the full-week mix is still unknown, and the Studio report for WebinarJam needs to be generated before next week’s review.
  5. The WebinarJam data landed late. WebinarJam sync kept failing on Tuesday because of a country-field bug, and it only finished on Wednesday at about 8:41am ET. The fix worked, but it is why this card is a day late. Keep an eye on the sync next week so the Tuesday card can go out on time.
